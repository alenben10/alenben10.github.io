I am a PhD student in Computational Biology at Cornell University. Before the PhD, I was a laboratory technician at Cornell, where I developed FuncMap, an LLM-assisted pipeline that extracts evidence-linked gene–metabolite–trait relations from full-text articles. Earlier, I worked on comparative genomics at UC Berkeley–USDA and on immunoinformatics, protein modeling, and metagenomics at Universidad Peruana Cayetano Heredia in Lima, Peru.
